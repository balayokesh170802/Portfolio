ABOUT THE PROJECT

ÆTHER is a self-initiated concept for a premium digital studio. The project explores a structured website experience that introduces the studio's philosophy, capabilities, working process, and services.

MY CONTRIBUTION

I designed the overall website experience, including the visual direction, page structure, content hierarchy, interface layouts, and responsive behaviour.

The project also served as an exploration of interaction, motion, and how individual sections could create a more engaging browsing experience.

I developed editorial layout grids, high-contrast serif and sans-serif typographic systems, and micro-interactions that communicate luxury digital craft.

I prototyped multi-device responsive adaptations in Figma, establishing reusable design tokens and fluid motion behaviors across all viewport breakpoints.
